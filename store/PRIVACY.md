# Privacy Policy — Binaural Waves

**Last updated:** 27 September 2026  
**Developer:** miferst-lab  
**App:** Binaural Waves (`com.miferstlab.binauralbeats`)

## What we collect
The app **does not collect, transmit or sell** any personal data.

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics, ads or trackers
- Settings (appearance, mix with other apps, selected sounds, volume, trial start date) stay on your device only
- In-app purchases (Premium) are processed by Google Play; we do not receive your payment details

## Permissions
- **Notifications** — optional, to show playback controls in the background
- **Foreground service / media playback** — so tones and ambient sounds keep playing with the screen off

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not profile users.

## Contact
Questions: via the repository [github.com/miferst-lab/binaural-beats](https://github.com/miferst-lab/binaural-beats) (Issues).
